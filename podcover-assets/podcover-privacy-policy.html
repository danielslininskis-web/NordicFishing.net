Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PodCover collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you visit our online store, place an order, contact us, or otherwise use our services.

PodCover is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. If you have questions or want to exercise your privacy rights, contact us at podcoverhelp@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the store, we may collect:

  • Contact information: your name, email address, telephone number, billing address, and delivery address.
  • Order information: the products you order, selected variants, order value, delivery method, returns, refunds, and communications about the order.
  • Payment information: payment status and transaction details. Full card details are normally processed by Shopify and the selected payment provider rather than stored directly by PodCover.
  • Device and usage information: IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, referring links, and information collected through cookies or similar technologies.
  • Messages: information you provide when contacting customer support or submitting a question.
  • Marketing preferences: whether you have agreed to receive marketing and whether you later unsubscribe.

How we use personal information

We use personal information to:

  • process, fulfil, deliver, and manage orders;
  • take payment and prevent fraudulent transactions;
  • provide customer support and respond to questions;
  • handle cancellations, returns, refunds, and complaints;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the store;
  • comply with accounting, tax, consumer-protection, and other legal obligations;
  • send marketing where you have consented or where otherwise permitted by law; and
  • establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Legal bases

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we process information when:

  • it is necessary to perform a contract with you, such as completing an order;
  • we must comply with a legal obligation;
  • we have a legitimate interest, such as securing and improving the store, provided your rights do not override that interest; or
  • you have given consent, such as for optional marketing cookies or newsletters.

Shopify

Our store is hosted by Shopify. Shopify processes information needed to provide the ecommerce platform, checkout, fraud prevention, and related services. Shopify may process information in countries outside your own. You can read more about Shopify’s privacy practices in Shopify’s own privacy policy.

Who we share information with

We may share relevant information with:

  • Shopify and companies that help operate the online store;
  • payment providers and fraud-prevention services;
  • delivery carriers and fulfilment providers;
  • email, analytics, hosting, and customer-support providers;
  • professional advisers such as accountants or legal advisers; and
  • public authorities where disclosure is required by law.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside Denmark or the European Economic Area. Where required, transfers are protected through an adequacy decision, approved contractual safeguards, or another lawful transfer mechanism.

Cookies

The store uses cookies and similar technologies for essential functions such as cart and checkout, and may use optional analytics or marketing technologies. Where required, optional cookies are used only after you make a choice through the cookie banner. You can change your browser settings or available cookie preferences, although blocking essential cookies may affect how the store works.

Marketing

If you subscribe to marketing, we may send news about products, drops, or store updates. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in an email or by contacting podcoverhelp@gmail.com. Unsubscribing from marketing does not stop essential order or service messages.

How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, including order fulfilment, customer support, accounting, tax, fraud prevention, and legal claims. Different information may be kept for different periods where required by law.

Security

We and our service providers use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational measures intended to protect personal information. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • request access to your personal information;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate information;
  • request deletion in certain circumstances;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive certain information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where processing relies on consent; and
  • complain to a data-protection authority.

To exercise a right, email podcoverhelp@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing the request.

If you are in Denmark, you may complain to Datatilsynet. You can find current contact information on the official Datatilsynet website.

Children

The store is not directed specifically at young children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a child who cannot legally provide it. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, providers, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact PodCover at podcoverhelp@gmail.com.
