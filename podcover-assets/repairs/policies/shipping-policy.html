Shipping policy

Shipping costs and destinations

Standard shipping is free to destinations offered at checkout. If an optional upgraded shipping service is available, its cost is shown before you pay. We only ship to the destinations available at checkout.

Dispatch

Where same-day dispatch is offered on the product page, it applies only to available items ordered before the displayed cutoff, normally 15:00 Copenhagen time Monday–Friday. Orders after the cutoff and orders placed at weekends are dispatched on the next working day. The dispatch date shown on the product page accounts for weekends and scheduled closures.

Dispatch means handing your parcel to the carrier. It is not a promise of same-day delivery. Sold-out variants cannot be ordered.

Delivery and updates

Order confirmation and dispatch information are sent to the email address used at checkout. Tracking information is included where the selected service provides it.

Delivery depends on the destination and carrier service. Any estimate shown at checkout or in the dispatch confirmation is an estimate unless a fixed delivery date is expressly agreed. We do not promise delivery times that have not been confirmed.

Your delivery address

Please provide a complete and accurate delivery address. If you notice an error, contact us promptly. We will try to help but cannot guarantee a change once an order has been dispatched.

Delayed, missing, or damaged parcels

Check any tracking information, then email podcoverhelp@gmail.com or use our contact form with your order number. We will look into the issue with the carrier. Your statutory rights for delayed, missing, or damaged delivery remain unaffected.
