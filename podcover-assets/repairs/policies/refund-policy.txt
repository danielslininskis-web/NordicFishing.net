Return and refund policy

For help with a PodCover order, email podcoverhelp@gmail.com or use our contact form (https://podcover.store/pages/podcover-contact). Include your order number.

Your cancellation window

For standard online purchases by customers in Denmark and the EU, you have 14 days after receiving your order to tell us you wish to cancel. Send a clear cancellation statement by email or through our contact form. We will provide return instructions and the return address. Your cancellation does not depend on waiting for our reply.

After notifying us, return the goods within the following 14 days.

Condition of returned goods

You may open ordinary packaging and inspect a case as you would in a shop. Opening the packaging does not automatically remove your cancellation right. Please include the accessories and original packaging where possible.

If you handle the goods more than necessary to inspect their nature, characteristics, and function, you may be responsible for the resulting loss in value. We may deduct that loss from your refund where the law permits.

Return postage

You pay the direct cost of return postage for a change-of-mind cancellation. We recommend a tracked service and keeping proof of postage until the return is complete. Contact us for the correct return instructions before sending a parcel.

If your item is faulty, damaged, or different from what you ordered, contact us before returning it. We will explain the next steps and cover any return costs that are legally our responsibility.

Personalised items

Goods made to your individual specifications or clearly personalised for you are generally excluded from change-of-mind cancellation. Choosing a standard colour or AirPods model does not by itself make a product personalised. Your statutory rights for faulty or incorrect goods remain unaffected.

Refunds

For an eligible cancellation, we refund the product price and any standard original delivery charge to the original payment method. Additional costs for a delivery upgrade chosen by you are not refunded.

We issue the refund within 14 days after receiving your cancellation notice. We may withhold it until we receive the returned goods or proof that they have been sent back, whichever happens first. Your bank or payment provider may need additional time to display the refund.

Faulty or incorrect orders

Contact podcoverhelp@gmail.com with your order number and a description of the problem. Clear photographs can help us assess it. Customers in Denmark generally have a two-year statutory right to complain about a lack of conformity; other mandatory rights may apply in your country.

This change-of-mind policy does not reduce your statutory rights for faulty, damaged, or incorrectly supplied goods.
